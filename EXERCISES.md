# Practice Exercises
1. Change the background color.
2. Draw a traffic light using rectangles and circles.
3. Draw a house with a chimney.
4. Rotate a square continuously.
5. Move a ball from left to right.
6. Move a rectangle with keyboard keys.
7. Modify DDA to draw negative slopes and all octants.
8. Compare DDA and Bresenham.
9. Draw a circle with the midpoint algorithm.
10. Create a scene containing at least five objects.
